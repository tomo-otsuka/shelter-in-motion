Ch. 2 — The Northwest

Seattle

WA · Aug 15 – 21, 2021 · 7 nights

A week in the city. After the quiet of the Oregon high desert, Seattle felt dense and alive, even with the residual quiet of the lockdown lingering in the damp air. I stayed with friends, turning their living room into a temporary basecamp.

The rhythm of the road paused for a moment. We drove out to Mt. Rainier one morning before sunrise, watching the mountain turn pink as the light crept over the cascades. The rest of the days were spent walking familiar streets that felt different now, an odd mixture of nostalgia and detachment.

The city was a halfway point. I had left San Francisco behind, but the real emptiness of the interior was still ahead of me. I spent a lot of time just watching the ferry boats cut across Puget Sound, getting ready for the next long push.
